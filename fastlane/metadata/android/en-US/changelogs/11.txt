Video Live Wallpaper 1.4.0

- Video looping is now disabled by default. Enable Loop video playback to repeat videos continuously; otherwise, videos play once and hold the final frame.
- Added optional video playback on phone unlock.
- Removed the crop-mode selector; video and GIF wallpapers always use Fill scaling to cover the screen edge to edge.
- Enabled R8 code shrinking and resource shrinking for release builds.
